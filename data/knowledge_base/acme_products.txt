Acme Corp offers three products: RouteMate, FleetTrack, and RetailSync.
RouteMate is the flagship logistics routing tool, launched in 2022.
FleetTrack is a fleet management dashboard released in 2023.
RetailSync connects retail point-of-sale systems to logistics, released in 2024.
RouteMate has over 10,000 active installations across India.
FleetTrack supports up to 500 vehicles per account.
Pricing: RouteMate starts at 2,999 rupees per month, FleetTrack at 4,999 rupees per month.
RetailSync pricing is custom-quoted based on store count.
All products run on Microsoft Azure cloud infrastructure.
