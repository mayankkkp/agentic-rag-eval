Acme Corp was founded in 2019 in Lucknow, India.
The company pivoted from a consulting model to SaaS in 2021.
RouteMate launched in 2022 with 50 beta customers.
Acme raised 2 million dollars in seed funding in 2022.
The company expanded to Delhi and Bengaluru offices in 2023.
Acme reached profitability in Q4 2024.
The company has not raised additional funding since the 2022 seed round.
